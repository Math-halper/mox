# Geometry and trigonometry

Geometry studies shape, size, position, and space. Trigonometry connects angles with side lengths, especially in triangles and circles.

## Angle facts

- A straight line is 180°; a full turn is 360°.
- Angles around a point add to 360°.
- Vertical (opposite) angles are equal.
- Complementary angles sum to 90°; supplementary angles sum to 180°.
- Parallel lines cut by a transversal create equal corresponding and alternate angles; same-side interior angles are supplementary.

## Triangles

Interior angles sum to 180°. Types: equilateral (all sides and angles equal, 60°), isosceles (two equal sides, base angles equal), scalene (all sides different). Right triangles have one 90° angle.

The exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles. The triangle inequality: any two sides must sum to more than the third.

Congruence tests: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and RHS (right angle, hypotenuse, side). Similar triangles have equal angles and proportional sides; corresponding lengths share the same scale factor, and areas scale by the square of it.

## Pythagoras and special triangles

For a right triangle with legs a and b and hypotenuse c: a² + b² = c². A 3-4-5 triangle satisfies 9 + 16 = 25. Common triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25.

45-45-90 triangles have sides in ratio 1 : 1 : √2. 30-60-90 triangles have sides in ratio 1 : √3 : 2.

## Perimeter, area, and volume

- Rectangle: P = 2(l + w), A = lw.
- Square: P = 4s, A = s².
- Parallelogram: A = bh.
- Triangle: A = ½bh.
- Trapezoid: A = ½(b₁ + b₂)h.
- Circle: circumference C = 2πr = πd, area A = πr². Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr; sector area = (θ/360) × πr².
- Rectangular prism: V = lwh, surface area = 2(lw + lh + wh).
- Cylinder: V = πr²h, lateral area = 2πrh.
- Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³, surface area = 4πr².
- Cone: V = (1/3)πr²h.
- Pyramid: V = (1/3) × base area × height.

## Coordinate geometry

- Distance between points: d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²).
- Midpoint: ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2).
- Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁).
- Circle centered at (h, k): (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r².

Transformations: translation (slide), rotation (turn), reflection (flip), dilation (resize). Rigid transformations — translations, rotations, reflections — preserve size and shape.

## Right-triangle trigonometry

For an acute angle θ:
- sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse
- cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse
- tan θ = opposite / adjacent
- SOH-CAH-TOA is the mnemonic.

Values to know: sin 30° = ½, cos 30° = √3/2, tan 30° = √3/3; sin 45° = cos 45° = √2/2, tan 45° = 1; sin 60° = √3/2, cos 60° = ½, tan 60° = √3.

Useful identities: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; tan θ = sin θ / cos θ.

## Law of sines and cosines

For any triangle with sides a, b, c opposite angles A, B, C:
- Law of sines: a / sin A = b / sin B = c / sin C.
- Law of cosines: c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C.
- Law of sines area formula: Area = ½ab sin C.

## Circles and radians

A radian is the angle that subtends an arc equal to the radius. 180° = π radians, so degrees × π/180 = radians. Chord, secant, tangent, and inscribed-angle facts: an angle inscribed in a semicircle is 90°; the angle at the center is twice the inscribed angle subtending the same arc.

## Solids and scaling

If every length is multiplied by k, area scales by k² and volume by k³. Doubling the radius of a sphere multiplies surface area by 4 and volume by 8.

## Units and conversions

- 1 inch = 2.54 cm; 1 foot = 12 inches; 1 yard = 3 feet; 1 mile = 5,280 feet ≈ 1.609 km.
- 1 m = 100 cm; 1 km = 1,000 m.
- 1 acre = 43,560 square feet; 1 hectare = 10,000 m².
- Area units square when converted: 1 m² = 10,000 cm².
