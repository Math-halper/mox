# Math formula and vocabulary reference

A compact reference for quickly looking up formulas, symbols, and terms.

## Symbols

- = equals; ≈ approximately equals; ≠ not equal
- < less than; > greater than; ≤ at most; ≥ at least
- ± plus or minus; ∑ sum; ∏ product; ∫ integral; √ square root
- π ≈ 3.14159; e ≈ 2.71828
- ∞ infinity; |x| absolute value; n! factorial; Δ change in
- ∠ angle; △ triangle; ⊥ perpendicular; ∥ parallel; ° degree
- ⇒ implies; ∈ is an element of; ∪ union; ∩ intersection

## Percentage and proportion

- Percent of a number: part = percent × whole.
- Percent change: (new − old)/old × 100.
- Simple interest: I = P·r·t.
- Compound interest: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt); continuous: A = Pe^(rt).
- Discount: sale price = original × (1 − discount rate).
- Tax or tip: total = subtotal × (1 + rate).
- Proportion: a/b = c/d ⇒ ad = bc.

## Distance, rate, time

d = rt. Average speed = total distance / total time (not the average of the speeds unless times are equal). Work rates add: if one pipe fills a pool in 4 hours and another in 6, together they fill 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12 of the pool per hour, so 12/5 = 2.4 hours.

## Mixtures and weighted averages

Weighted average = Σ(weight × value) / Σ weights. For a 20% brine mixed with a 50% brine in a 3:1 ratio, the result is (3×20 + 1×50)/4 = 27.5%.

## Financial growth

- Rule of 72: doubling time ≈ 72 / annual percent rate.
- Inflation adjusted value: future cost = present cost × (1 + rate)^years.
- Loan payment: M = P·i(1 + i)ⁿ / ((1 + i)ⁿ − 1), where i is the monthly rate and n the number of payments.

## Geometry quick list

## Statistics quick list

Mean = Σx/n. Sample standard deviation s = √(Σ(x − x̄)²/(n − 1)). z = (x − μ)/σ. Margin of error ≈ z*·s/√n. Expected value = Σx·P(x). Combination C(n, r) = n!/(r!(n − r)!).

## Unit conversions

- Length: 1 in = 2.54 cm; 1 ft = 0.3048 m; 1 mi = 1.609 km; 1 km = 0.621 mi.
- Mass: 1 lb = 0.4536 kg; 1 kg = 2.205 lb; 1 oz = 28.35 g.
- Volume: 1 gal (US) = 3.785 L; 1 cup = 236.6 mL; 1 L = 0.264 gal.
- Temperature: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9; K = °C + 273.15.
- Energy: 1 calorie = 4.184 joules; 1 kWh = 3.6 million joules.
- Data: 1 KB = 1,024 bytes; 1 MB = 1,024 KB; 1 GB = 1,024 MB; 1 TB = 1,024 GB.

## Vocabulary

- Integer: whole number including negatives.
- Rational: can be written as a fraction of integers.
- Irrational: cannot, such as √2 or π.
- Prime: exactly two factors.
- Variable, coefficient, constant, term, expression, equation.
- Function, domain, range, intercept, slope, vertex.
- Factorial: n! = n × (n − 1) × … × 1, with 0! = 1.
- Reciprocal: 1/x. Absolute value: distance from zero.
- Sum, difference, product, quotient.
